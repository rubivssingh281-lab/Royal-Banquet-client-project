Our The Royal Banquet

The Royal Banquet Palace is perfectly located in a prime and easily accessible area—opposite Dwarkadham Apartment, Pochanpur Colony, Dwarka, Delhi—making it a convenient choice for all your guests.

With a beautiful ambiance and a royal touch, our banquet is designed to make your special occasions truly unforgettable. The venue is fully air-conditioned, offers ample parking space, is Wi-Fi enabled, and comes with 100% power backup, ensuring a smooth, uninterrupted experience for every celebration or corporate event.
