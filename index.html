Heartfelt Hospitality

Built for Family, Designed for Cherished Memories

A wedding is far more than a single day — it is the joyful union of two lineages, histories, and generations. We have curated every touchpoint with your entire family in mind.

Elder-Friendly Comfort & Accessibility

Flat, step-free pathways, spacious elevator transitions, ergonomic cushioned seating, and serene quiet tea alcoves ensure that beloved grandparents and senior family members celebrate in total comfort.

Honoring Sacred Customs & Heritage

Whether you are organizing a majestic Baraat, traditional Pheras, Sangeet performances, Haldi rituals, or unity vows, our flexible spaces adapt seamlessly to honor your family’s unique rituals and cultural traditions.

Heirloom Multi-Cuisine Banqueting

Food is at the heart of every family blessing. Our master culinary team crafts authentic pure vegetarian, Jain, Awadhi, Punjabi, and international spreads, even incorporating your family's cherished regional recipes.

Private Family & Bridal Sanctuaries

Expansive, private climate-controlled suites complete with vanity mirrors and plush lounges where parents, brides, and grooms can relax, share quiet prayers, and capture intimate family moments before the grand entrance.

Our Legacy

Over 15 Years of Bringing Families Together

⚜

At The Royal Banquet Palace, our journey began with an enduring belief: life’s greatest blessings happen when families gather to celebrate love. Inspired by the timeless philosophy of Atithi Devo Bhava, we treat every celebration with the reverence of our own family milestone.

Over the last decade and a half, we have had the honor of hosting thousands of multi-generational weddings, silver and golden wedding anniversaries, and sacred ceremonies. Every hall is bathed in pure bridal white and warm champagne light, creating a regal atmosphere that feels just like home.

"From the first tearful embrace from a grandparent to the energetic dancing of the children — we are proud to be the canvas where your family's legacy unfolds."
